For hosts & co-hosts

For people who look after holiday homes, often for someone else

The Co-Host Pro app is made for hosts, co-hosts and managers who run short-term rentals for owners, in Dubai and in South Africa. It was built by one, for that job.

Who Co-Host Pro serves

Holiday-home managers

You manage units for several owners and send each one a monthly statement.

  • Fee settings per property: management %, channel fees, cleaning fee
  • One statement per property per month, locked once sent
  • Owners get a read-only portal with their own figures

Airbnb co-hosts

You co-host listings where Airbnb may pay part of the payout straight to the owner.

  • Import Airbnb reservation breakdowns, matched by confirmation code
  • Co-host payouts recorded on the booking
  • Statements show what Airbnb already paid the owner, so the balance is right

Hosts with their own homes

You run your own properties, perhaps in more than one country.

  • One calendar across Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo, synced by iCal
  • Income per property, month by month
  • AED and ZAR kept separate
A normal day

What the Co-Host Pro app does for you, day to day

  1. Morning: the Today screen shows check-ins and check-outs, bookings to verify and owner statements due.
  2. Turnovers: each checkout has a cleaning task. Assign it, the cleaner completes it, you approve it, and the cost is recorded once.
  3. New bookings: stays arrive from your channels' iCal calendar links. Enter or import the guest total and the breakdown is worked out for you.
  4. Month end: verify any open bookings, generate statements, send them and mark them paid. Sent months lock.
For your owners

Give owners a clear, read-only view

Each owner logs in with a one-time code and sees only their own properties: an overview, the calendar and the statements you've published. Draft or unverified income is not shown.

  • Owners can't change anything.
  • They never see other owners' properties.
  • Statements show the bookings and every expense charged to them.
Where

Made for Dubai and South Africa

Dubai

VAT and Tourism Dirham per booking, Tourism Dirham rate per property, guest ID and DET registration status, cleaning rate card by unit size, AED throughout.

South Africa

ZAR properties with their own fee settings and cleaning arrangements, including homes with no cleaning company, and statements in ZAR. VAT and tourism charges can be switched off per property.
