Why Co-Host Pro

Built for the jobs generic tools get wrong in Dubai

The Co-Host Pro app was built by a working Dubai holiday-home operator. It focuses on getting the money and the local rules right, every time.

Dubai rules built in

VAT and Tourism Dirham are worked out from the guest total on every booking. Guest ID and DET (formerly DTCM) registration status sit on each Dubai stay.

Owner statements that are right

One statement per property per month from one calculation. It's locked once sent, every change is audit-logged, and co-host payouts already paid are shown as paid.

AED and ZAR, never mixed

Each property keeps its own currency. Totals are shown per currency, and no exchange rate is ever guessed.

Day-to-day operations

iCal calendar sync with Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo. Cleaning board and cleaning costs, booking verification, income per property and an owner portal.

One calendar

Every stay, every channel, one view

Co-Host Pro reads your Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo calendars through their iCal links and shows every stay, block and owner stay on one multi-property calendar, each channel in its own colour.

  • Bookings and blocked dates are read from each listing's iCal calendar link about every 15 minutes.
  • Possible double bookings, missing stays and date changes are flagged for you to check.
  • A checkout clean is created automatically for each booking.
Money you can show an owner

From guest total to owner payout, the same way every time

Enter what the guest paid. Co-Host Pro takes out VAT and Tourism Dirham, the channel fee, the management fee and the cleaning fee, using each property's own fee settings, and shows the owner's share.

  • Statements can't be generated until every booking in the month is verified.
  • Sent and paid months are locked. Corrections go into an open month.
  • Every add, edit and delete is in the activity log with old and new values.
