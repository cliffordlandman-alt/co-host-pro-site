About

Built by a working Dubai holiday-home operator

The Co-Host Pro app is the property management system we built to run the holiday homes we look after. It's built and used by the people who run the homes.

The company

Co-Host Pro looks after short-term rentals: holiday homes managed in Dubai and Airbnb homes in Gqeberha, South Africa. To run them we built the Co-Host Pro app, property management software for short-term rental hosts, co-hosts and managers, focused on Dubai and South Africa. The app runs that portfolio every day.

We're in early access. We aren't offering public sign-up yet. We're working with a small number of operators whose needs match what the Co-Host Pro app does well today.

The founder

Co-Host Pro was founded by {{brand.founderName}}, a South African based in Dubai. He manages holiday homes in Dubai for their owners and runs Airbnb homes in Gqeberha.

Running those homes meant working out Dubai VAT and Tourism Dirham on every booking, sending owners statements they could rely on, handling Airbnb co-host payouts, and keeping AED and ZAR properties apart. The Co-Host Pro app was built to do those jobs properly. Every finished feature on this site is used in that business.

What we care about

  • Correct numbers. One calculation for every booking, statements locked once sent, and currencies that are never mixed.
  • Honesty about what's built. If something isn't finished, we say so.
  • Respect for data. Guest and owner data is used only to run the homes it belongs to. See Security & data.
