Dubai compliance

Dubai's rules, worked out on every booking

  • VAT taken out of the guest total. The 5% VAT included in what the guest paid is worked out per booking, not added on top.
  • Tourism Dirham per bedroom per night. Charged per bedroom per night up to 30 nights, at the rate set for each property, so Standard and Deluxe units can differ.
  • Rates set once per property. Management %, channel fee, VAT and tourism settings live in one place per property. Verified bookings keep the rates they were saved with.
  • Guest ID and DET registration status. Each booking shows whether ID is pending or collected. Airbnb guests show "Verified by Airbnb", and Dubai stays carry a reminder that DET (formerly DTCM) guest registration still needs a passport copy.
  • Holiday-home permit tracking In development
Owner statements

Statements that are always right, and stay right

Owners trust numbers that don't move. Co-Host Pro keeps one source of truth from booking to payout.

One source of truth

Owner statements, income per property and the company statement all use the same calculation. One statement per property per month: a duplicate can't be created.

Locked once sent

When a statement is sent or paid, its bookings and charges can't be changed. A correction goes into an open month, and the message says which month and property.

Every change audit-logged

Booking edits and every income or expense added, edited or deleted are written to the activity log with old and new values. Private iCal links and passwords are blanked out of the log.

Co-host payouts shown as paid

Where Airbnb pays a co-host payout straight to the owner, the statement shows it as already paid and the balance is what's left, so nothing is paid twice.

Says what's missing

If a statement can't be produced, Co-Host Pro lists exactly why: bookings that still need the guest total verified, or properties with no owner linked.

Clear and printable

Expenses charged to the owner are itemised line by line. "Print / Save PDF" opens a clean page with just that statement.

Multi-currency

AED and ZAR side by side, never added together

  • Each property has its own currency: AED for Dubai units, ZAR for South African homes.
  • Totals are always shown per currency. AED and ZAR are never added, and no exchange rate is used.
  • A month that would mix currencies on one statement is held for you to check, never guessed.
  • Owner charges must be in the property's own currency.
Operations

The daily work, in one place

iCal calendar sync

Reads Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo calendars through each listing's iCal calendar link. Stays and blocked dates appear on the month view and the multi-property calendar, each channel in its own colour.

Cleaning workflow

A checkout clean for every booking. Assign, complete and approve, with photos. Cleaning costs are recorded once per clean from a rate card by unit size, or a property's own rate.

Verify bookings

One screen lists every booking still missing its guest total. Import Airbnb reservation breakdowns from a CSV, matched by confirmation code, with a preview before anything changes.

Income per property

Each property's month or year: bookings, nights, occupancy, gross income, taxes, channel fees, cleaning, management fee and owner share. Unverified bookings are marked as estimates.

Owner portal

Owners log in to a read-only portal with a one-time code and see only their own properties: overview, calendar and published statements.

Team roles

Admin, manager, reservations, accountant, operations, cleaner and maintenance roles, each limited to what that job needs.

Income and expenses

One form for company expenses, owner-charged expenses and other income, with categories and a revenue-stream tag. Entries can be edited in place, with history.

Maintenance

Tickets with priorities, costs and who pays: the owner or the company. Owner-charged repairs flow through to the statement.

Mobile-friendly

Every screen is laid out for a phone, with a bottom bar for the main jobs. Long lists load page by page.

How the channel connection works today: the Co-Host Pro app reads each listing's iCal calendar link (the calendar export link) from Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo. It brings in stays and blocked dates. It does not change your listings, prices or availability on those channels, and iCal links carry no amounts, which is why guest totals are verified in the app. It does not read or copy Airbnb listing pages.
