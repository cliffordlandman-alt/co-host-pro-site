1. Who we are

This policy covers the Co-Host Pro website and the Co-Host Pro property management software, provided by {{company.legalName}} ("Co-Host Pro", "we", "us"). Our registered address is {{company.registeredAddress}}. We are based in {{brand.baseCity}}.

Our Information Officer under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) is {{privacy.popiaInformationOfficer}}.

2. Our role

When a business (a host, co-host or property manager) uses Co-Host Pro, that business decides what data to put in and why. For that data, the business is responsible for it (the "controller" or "responsible party") and we process it on its behalf. Guests and owners with questions about their data should contact the business that manages their stay or property first. We'll help that business respond.

We are responsible for data we collect for our own purposes, such as messages sent to us and our customers' account details.

Today the Co-Host Pro app is used to run the holiday homes we look after in Dubai and our Airbnb homes in Gqeberha, South Africa. For that portfolio, we are the business responsible for guest and owner data.

3. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or third-party scripts. It has no forms. If you email or call us, we use your details only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for a short time for security and to keep the site running.

4. Data in Co-Host Pro

  • Customer and staff accounts: name, email, role and a hashed password, plus a record of logins and changes made.
  • Property owners: name, email, phone and address if given, the properties they own, statements and payout records.
  • Guests: name, contact details where provided by the booking channel or the host, stay dates, number of guests, booking channel and reference, booking amounts, ID status, and notes the host adds.
  • Properties and operations: property details, cleaning and maintenance tasks, cleaning photos, expenses and income records.
  • Channel data: data from iCal calendar links and from files the host chooses to import. See section 8.

5. How we use it

  • To provide Co-Host Pro: calendars, cleaning, booking finances, Dubai VAT and Tourism Dirham, owner statements and reports.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent misuse and keep an activity log of changes.
  • To support customers and fix problems.
  • To meet legal duties, such as keeping tax and accounting records.

We don't sell personal data and we don't use it for advertising.

6. Legal bases

Depending on where you are, we rely on: performing our contract with customers; legal obligations, such as tax and record-keeping rules; legitimate interests, such as securing the service and answering enquiries, where these aren't outweighed by your rights; and consent where the law requires it. We aim to comply with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and, where it applies, the EU and UK GDPR.

7. Sharing

  • Within a customer's account: with that customer's team according to their roles, and with owners for their own properties' published statements.
  • Service providers: companies that host or support Co-Host Pro for us, such as hosting and, when a customer switches them on, email or messaging delivery and optional AI tools. They may use the data only to provide their service to us. We'll give our current list on request.
  • Legal reasons: where the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or our legal rights.
  • Business changes: if Co-Host Pro is reorganised or sold, under the same protections.

8. Airbnb and other channel data

Booking data from Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo is used only to provide the host's own services, never for advertising, never sold and never combined across hosts. If Co-Host Pro is approved for Airbnb's API, data received through it will be handled as described on How we use Airbnb and other channel data and in line with Airbnb's API Terms, and deleted when it's no longer needed or when the host disconnects.

9. International transfers

We serve customers in the UAE and South Africa, and our service providers may process data in other countries. Where data moves across borders, we use the safeguards the law requires, such as contractual protections. Co-Host Pro data is hosted in {{hosting.dataRegion}}.

10. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires. For example, hosts must keep accounting records, and Dubai guest records for a set period. When a customer leaves, we delete or return their data within 30 days, apart from records we must keep by law.

11. Security

We protect data with measures including hashed passwords, expiring signed sessions, role-based access, a read-only owner portal and an activity log. See Security & data for what's in place today and what we commit to.

12. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how it's used, to receive a copy in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. If your data is held by a business that uses Co-Host Pro, please contact that business first. You can also complain to your data protection regulator, for example the UAE Data Office or South Africa's Information Regulator.

13. Children

Co-Host Pro is a business tool and isn't meant for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect children's data for our own purposes.

14. Changes

We'll update this policy when our practices change and show the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll tell customers directly.
